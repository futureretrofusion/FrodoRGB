# Notices and attribution

## Upstream

FrodoRGB is derived from **Frodo V4.1b**, the Commodore 64 emulator originally
written by **Christian Bauer**.

Future Retro Fusion does not claim authorship of the original Frodo emulator.

## Future Retro Fusion

The FRF source lineage adds AmigaOS 68k work including CyberGraphX RTG output,
native RGB/PAL display work, AHI lifecycle changes, AmigaDOS startup/media
integration, cartridge mapping work, VIC multiplexer fixes and the dedicated
FrodoRGB performance path.
